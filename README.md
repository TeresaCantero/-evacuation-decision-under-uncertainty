# Evacuation Decision Under Uncertainty

This project explores how evacuation decisions are made under conditions of incomplete and unreliable information, particularly in conflict settings.

In high-risk environments, decision-makers must act without full knowledge of population conditions, route safety, or communication reliability. This model provides a simplified way to examine how different levels of uncertainty affect evacuation outcomes.

The goal is not to predict real-world evacuations, but to better understand how uncertainty influences decision-making and how concepts such as precaution and proportionality may be interpreted in practice.

## Project Structure

- `model.py`: A simple simulation of evacuation outcomes under different conditions
- Scenarios vary based on communication reliability, risk levels, and route availability

## Purpose

This repository is part of a broader research project examining the intersection of artificial intelligence, decision-making under uncertainty, and civilian protection.
